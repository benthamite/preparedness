## 1. Electricity
- US SAIDI 2024: 662.6 min with major event days (MED), 131.6 without; SAIFI 1.53 / 1.07. 2025: 269.2 / 125.3 min. 2015–2023 with-MED range 209–506 min. EIA Table 11.1 (IEEE method), https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/html/epa_11_01.html — V
- 2024: 11 h/customer, ~9 h from major events (2014–2023 average ~4 h); South Carolina ~53 h. https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=66744 (Dec 2025) — V
- AHS 2023: 25.4% of households had a complete outage in 12 months (urban 22.8%, rural 35.4%; Detroit 44.8%, Miami 5.8%); 70% of those had one ≥6 h (17.7% of all households); 2.9M (2.2%) stayed away overnight; 4.9M lost food. https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2024/10/power-outages.html — V
- **Tail, best source**: NREL/PNNL 2022 (Ericson et al., EAGLE-I + EIA-861, 2015–2021). Per-customer recurrence, contiguous US: 12–24 h 67 yr; 1–2 d 59; 2–3 d 127; 3–5 d 152; 5–7 d 495; >1 wk 790 (South 290; West 1,479). Derived annual probability: >24 h 3.5%; >72 h 1.0%; >5 d 0.33%; >1 wk 0.13% (South 0.34%). Conditional on a major-event outage: 36% >1 d, 9.9% >3 d, 1.3% >7 d (hurricanes: 52% / 33% / 2.5%). https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy23osti/83092.pdf (read via PNNL-33340 mirror) — V
- >2 weeks: not found. Derive from EAGLE-I county series or DOE OE-417 restoration times; naive extrapolation of NREL's curve gives ~0.005%/yr, certainly too thin-tailed (Maria, Helene).
- Conflict to resolve: RECS reports 18% of households (22.7M) with an outage ≥24 h in 2020 and 22% (28.7M) in 2024 (Louisiana 60%/49%, Maine 47%/54%, Florida 15%/38%, Texas 20%/33%, New York 17%/11%). Table gives no reference period. That is ~5x NREL and hard to square with SAIDI; I would treat it as an upper bound until the questionnaire wording is checked. https://www.eia.gov/consumption/residential/data/2020/state/pdf/State%20Structural%20Characteristics.pdf and .../2024/state/pdf/STstructural_2024.pdf — V (numbers), reference period not found
- Do et al. 2023: 17,484 county-level 8+ h outages in 2018–2020; 62% coincide with extreme weather. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-38084-6 — S
- Europe: CEER 7th report PDF too large to fetch; country values not found. CEER 6.1: unplanned SAIDI incl. exceptional events 9–371 min across countries (2016). https://ceer.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/C18-EQS-86-03_Benchmarking_Report_6.1.pdf — S. Typical Western Europe 10–70 min/yr — R. Iberian blackout 28 Apr 2025: 50–60M people, ~10 h for most, Spain fully back <23 h. https://www.euronews.com/2025/04/29/power-returns-after-massive-blackout-in-spain-and-portugal-as-fallout-continues — S
- Buenos Aires: ENRE semester Sep 2024–Feb 2025: Edesur missed SAIDI and SAIFI targets in every district; Edenor missed SAIDI in three. https://www.argentina.gob.ar/enre/calidad-del-servicio/16a-revision-semestral — V. Edesur annual SAIDI 19.5 h (2023), 16.4 h (2024). https://www.cronista.com/negocios/aunque-perdio-29100-millones-en-2024-edesur-ve-un-claro-proceso-de-mejora-en-el-mercado — S. 16 Jun 2019 national blackout: ~48M people, 6–14 h — S. Multi-day heat-wave outages (Feb–Mar 2023, per ENRE audit) — S. So Buenos Aires runs 3–10x the US ex-major-event figure, with a summer-peaked tail.

No national population-affected or duration totals. https://19january2025snapshot.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-01/10586_boil-water-advisories_final_rtc_20240603_admin.pdf — V

- Household annual probability: not found. Derive from state advisory databases weighted by population served.

- Jackson 2022: citywide boil notice lifted 15 Sep after ~7 weeks. https://www.npr.org/2022/09/15/1123264927/jackson-miss-boil-water-lifted — S. ~150,000 residents, ~1 week with little pressure — R

## 3. Natural hazards
- Displacement (Census Household Pulse, Dec 2022–Oct 2023): 1.3% of adults per year. By duration: <1 wk 0.6%; 1 wk–1 mo 0.3%; 1–6 mo 0.1%; >6 mo 0.1%; never returned 0.2%. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12374215 — V
- 2022: 3.4M adults (1.4%); about half from hurricanes; Louisiana 11%, Florida 5%. IDMC's 2008–2021 average is only 800,000/yr. https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/disasters-displaced-more-than-3-million-americans-in-2022/ — V
- Annual probability of being under an evacuation order: not found. Milton 2024: mandatory orders in counties with 5.9M people — S. Derive from state order archives.
- Earthquake: ~75% of the US could see damaging shaking within 100 years; California >95%. https://www.usgs.gov/news/national-news-release/new-usgs-map-shows-where-damaging-earthquakes-are-most-likely-occur-us (2024) — S
- Flood: 14.6M properties (10.3%) at substantial risk. First Street 2020, https://www.preventionweb.net/publications/view/72504 — S
- Heat: 13.2M households (10%; urban 10.6%) uncomfortably hot ≥24 h in 2023; in air-conditioned homes 4.9M from equipment breakdown, 1.3M from power outage. https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2025/09/heat-risks-cooling-problems.html — V
- Wildfire and winter-storm household rates: not found. Texas 2021: ~4.5M customers out, many 2–4 days — R
- Concentration is extreme: state displacement rates span roughly 10x.

## 5. Nuclear war
- Rodriguez 2019: ~1.1%/yr any nuclear war; 0.38%/yr US–Russia. Inputs: Barrett 2013 0.9%/yr inadvertent US–Russia (90% CI 0.02–7%); Good Judgment 2018 0.40%/yr any state attack with a fatality, 0.01%/yr US–Russia. https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/PAYa6on5gJKwAywrF — V
- Samotsvety Mar 2022: NATO–Russia exchange ~0.8%/yr; P(London hit | exchange) 18%; 24 micromorts/month for London, other major cities 1.5–2x lower. https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/KRFXjCqqfGQAYirm5 — V. Oct 2022 update: post-first-use escalation beyond Ukraine 0.39%/month; London figure not recovered (samotsvety.org down).
- XPT 2022: use causing >1,000 deaths by 2030: superforecasters 4%, experts 4.5%; nuclear catastrophe (>10% of humanity) by 2030: 0.5% / 1%. https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/YyBoSSaWpNacLnCji — S
- Metaculus: "detonated as an act of war by 2050" 34%, 535 forecasters (~1.8%/yr), https://www.metaculus.com/questions/4779/ — V. "Reach 2035 without a non-test detonation" 76%, 196 forecasters (~3.3%/yr for any detonation), https://www.metaculus.com/questions/3150/ — V. Fatal non-state detonation by 2030: 1%, https://www.metaculus.com/questions/7406/ — V
- Ord: ~1 in 1,000 existential risk per century — R
- NATO city: large-scale exchange 0.01–0.4%/yr depending on source; chance a given major city is struck ≈ that times ~0.2–0.5 (derived, weak).
- Buenos Aires: direct strike risk ≈ 0 (judgment). Exposure shifts to famine and trade collapse: Xia et al. 2022 estimate >5 billion famine deaths after a US–Russia war. https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-022-00573-0 — S. Argentina and Australia stay calorie-sufficient in that paper — R. So food stocks matter less there than trade and financial resilience.

## 7. Economic and financial
