Two requested sources could not be read directly, and about half the prices are unverified estimates. Details are in the last section.

**Source codes**
- **TP**: The Prepared (beginner checklist, bug-out-bag list, home medical list, water, food and respirator reviews).
- **RG**: Ready.gov (kit, car, plan, financial, radiation pages).
- **RC**: American Red Cross.
- **WC\***: Wirecutter, not fetched; the asterisk marks picks named in Wirecutter Show episode notes.
- **CDC**: Prepare Your Health, emergency water and potassium iodide pages.
- **BBK**: German checklist PDF and web pages.
- **CH**: Swiss "Kluger Rat – Notvorrat".
- **SE**: Swedish brochure "In case of crisis or war" plus mcf.se pages.
- **FI**: Finnish 72-hour brochure.
- **UK**: gov.uk "Prepare" (added).
- **NO**: Norwegian DSB (added).
- **NPG**: "Nuclear Preparedness Guide" (LessWrong / EA Forum, 2022).
- **NDP**: "Accrue Nuclear Dignity Points" (LessWrong, 2022), including comments.
- **JK**: Jeff Kaufman's posts on reusable masks, elastomeric fitting, mini-PAPR and far-UVC (2024–25).
- **PPP**: "Personal Pandemic Preparedness" (EA Forum, 2025).

**Price codes**
- **NBC**: NBC Select emergency-kit roundup, updated 17 Nov 2026.

- **mfr**: the manufacturer's own site.
- **est.**: unverified estimate.

### (a) Items nearly every source agrees on
Stored water; shelf-stable food; battery or crank radio; flashlight with spare batteries; first-aid kit; prescription medicines plus basic over-the-counter drugs; cash in small notes; power bank; hygiene supplies (toilet paper, wipes, sanitiser, bin bags); blankets or sleeping bags and warm clothes; document copies; paper contact list and household plan; masks; matches. A camping stove is universal in the European lists and The Prepared but absent from the US government lists.

### (b) Specialised or rationalist/EA-only items
- Elastomeric respirator with P100 filters, and fit testing.
- PAPR.
- Far-UVC lamp.
- HEPA purifier (in no government kit list).
- Goggles, gloves and gowns for pandemics.
- Geiger counter or dosimeter.
- A year or more of stored food.
- EMP shielding.
- Evacuation tripwires and relocation abroad.
- Full-face CBRN mask, emergency antibiotics, bleeding-control kit and satellite messenger (The Prepared only).
- Potassium iodide is not EA-only: Finland and Norway list it officially.

### Gaps and friction
- **Wirecutter not read.** nytimes.com is blocked for both fetch and search. WC* entries come only from episode notes on a podcast mirror, so Wirecutter's quantities and "skip" advice are missing.
- **Red Cross read indirectly.** redcross.org returned 403. The list comes from a municipal mirror of the current page plus an older Red Cross checklist PDF.
- **Swedish brochure is the June 2026 reprint** (MCF0180), not the November 2024 print. I did not compare the two editions.
- **Swiss calculator and full BBK guide PDF were unreadable.** I used the Swiss advice page and the official BBK checklist PDF.
- **Many manufacturer shops rate-limited the fetcher,** which is why so many prices are "est.". The Prepared's review prices date from 2019–2022.
- **Cooling is nearly absent** from every surveyed list.
- **Two rows rest on thin sourcing.** Row 4 (bathtub bladder) is in no fetched source, and row 76 (air purifier) appears only in comment threads.
